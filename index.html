Simulator system · Demo · 2026

            
Pet Hatching & Following

            
Eggs, a cinematic hatch reveal, and pets that follow you and boost your coins.

            
    
              
  • Every roll happens on the server, using the same odds shown on the egg
    • 
              
  • Pet ownership and the equip limit are checked on the server
    • 
              
  • Followers are drawn on each player's device, so they cost the server nothing
    • 
              
  • Cheat-tested: fake pet IDs, junk data and spam are rejected
    • 
            

            
Credits
The pet models, UI design, icons and sound effect in this clip are third-party assets and are not my own work. All scripting and systems shown are by Phantom Studio.

          

        


        

          

            
            
          

          

            
Shop & saving · Demo · 2026

            
Exploit-proof Shop

            
A shop that saves every purchase, with a live exploit tester trying to break it.

            
    
              
  • The client sends only an item ID; prices and limits live on the server
    • 
              
  • Fake prices, made-up items and edited coins are all rejected
    • 
              
  • Spam is rate-limited, and 10 buys at once charge you exactly once
    • 
              
  • Session-locked saving, confirmed before the "saved" badge shows
    • 
            

            
Credits
The pet model in the Extra Pet Slot icon is a third-party asset, and saving uses ProfileStore by loleris (open source, Apache 2.0). All scripting, systems and UI shown are by Phantom Studio.

          

        


        

          

            
            
          

          

            
Combat system · Demo · 2026 · Sound on

            
Sword Combat & Ragdoll

            
A four-hit sword combo with a ragdoll finisher, tested against live cheat attempts.

            
    
              
  • The server picks the combo step, the timing and what gets hit
    • 
              
  • A hitbox swept across the swing, with range, facing and wall checks
    • 
              
  • 30 swings sent at once deal one swing's damage
    • 
              
  • Ragdolls that knock down and get back up cleanly
    • 
            

            
Credits
The sword model and aura ("Sword Auras" by RexR_Reborn) and the sword animations are third-party assets, and the sound effects are from Pro Sound Effects via Roblox. All scripting and systems shown are by Phantom Studio.

          03
          
How a commission works

        

        

          

          
    
            
  1. Brief
    You describe what you want. I ask the questions that usually cause rework later: what saves, who sees what, which platforms.
    2. 
            
  2. Quote
    A written scope, price and timeline before any work starts. No surprises.
    3. 
            
  3. Build & test
    Written and playtested in Studio on server and client, including the ways a cheater would try to break it.
    4. 
            
  4. Deliver
    Scripts plus setup notes: where each one goes, what it needs, and anything it deliberately doesn't do.
    5. 
          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          04
          
Good questions

        

        

          
How much does a commission cost?
            
Every project is quoted individually after a short brief, because a simple shop and a full combat system are very different jobs. Message me and you'll get a written quote.

          
Can you work inside my existing game?
            
Yes. I work within your current structure and follow your code style, so the new system feels like it was always there.

          
Can you fix exploits in scripts I already have?
            
Yes. A review ranks problems exploits first, then data loss, then performance, then everything else.

          
How long does it take?
            
It depends on scope. The timeline is part of the written quote, before you commit to anything.

          
How do I pay?
            
Payment terms are agreed with you before work starts.
